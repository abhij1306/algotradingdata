## What we now know
1. **Setups (VCP, cheat, Darvas, EMA pullback) have no stock-picking edge.** Their in-sample edge was the
   full-day-volume look-ahead (+0.18..0.43R/trade). Honest entries: old VCP +0.1..0.8%/trade over TRI
   (t<0.8); v3 with volume rule −5%/trade. → Stop researching setups as an alpha source.
2. **Momentum rotation is clean and the signal is real** (independent rebuild matches the engine; beats
   equal-weight NIFTY 500 by ~13 pts/yr before costs, also in 2012–15). Realistic excess over TRI
   +2..5 pts/yr. 80% of 2016–21 growth came from 2020-04..2021-12; levers we tuned are smaller than
   rebalance-day luck (25.1–31.0 CAGR by shifting the day).
3. **Drawdowns are mostly market beta** (60–90% of each large drawdown), plus selling fallen leaders near
   the low in sharp V-shaped falls (2025). Stops and intra-month exits on the rotation do not help.
4. **Value alone had no premium 2016–21** and its drawdowns are larger than momentum's; quality
   (ROCE, low debt, CFO>0) had the lowest drawdowns. Value is best as a filter/tie-break inside momentum
   (drop the most expensive quintile). Fundamentals data is incomplete before 2020 and biased toward
   companies that survived to 2020.
