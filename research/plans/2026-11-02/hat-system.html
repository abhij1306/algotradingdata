HAT momentum system

HAT is a rules-based system that holds the 20 strongest NIFTY 500 stocks and rebalances once a month. This page sets out what it reads, the rules it follows, what every backtest reports, and how the current strategy (called c1) has done. It trades on paper only; real orders need the owner's approval.

How it works

From data to orders

DataDaily and 5-minute prices, corporate actions, index membership, costs
UniverseNIFTY 500 members on that date, liquid and priced at ₹50 or more
RankingRisk-adjusted 6- and 12-month momentum, every month-end close
PortfolioTop 20, kept while still in the top 40, weighted by inverse beta
OrdersTrade at 09:20 next session; never at the 09:15 open
ReviewOwner approves trades; a switch moves between manual and automatic

Strategy parameters

The c1 rules

c1 has no stop losses and no profit targets. A stock leaves the book when its rank falls out of the top 40, or on a demerger, delisting or suspension. The book stays about 99% invested.

Ground rules

Rules every test follows

  • Stocks only, NIFTY 500 only; no ETFs in any strategy.
  • Data from 2011 onwards, point in time, adjusted for dividends and corporate actions.
  • Demerged stocks are exited, with the gap handled explicitly.
  • No fills before 09:20; same-day exits finish before the 15:15 closing auction.
  • Every year is judged against the NIFTY 500 TRI; a large shortfall means something is wrong.
  • Idle cash counts as a failure; deployment is reported first.
  • The stored market database is read-only for backtests.
  • Live trading stays on paper until the owner approves a frozen version.
