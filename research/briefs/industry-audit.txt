Industry history and data-audit additions, branch hat/industry-audit.
Goal: every NIFTY 500 member-day 2016+ has a point-in-time industry so by-industry breakdowns and the per-industry cap mean something, and the data audit catches the data issues found on 2026-10-26.
Read first: /data/workspace/audits/2026-10-26-backtest-review-fable.md (H4) and /data/workspace/audits/2026-10-26-data-review-fable.md (F1, F3, F4).
Outcomes:
1. Industry history (orchestrator's rule, put it in docs/decisions.md): one taxonomy, the current NSE NIFTY 500 list "Industry" column (as in hat_industry today). Each instrument chain takes its industry from the newest NIFTY 500 list snapshot that lists its ISIN in that taxonomy (snapshots: /data/workspace/nifty500-history/raw/wayback/*.csv and raw/ind_nifty500list_*.csv; copy what the code needs under HAT_DATA_ROOT/admin, never into the repo if it is data); names only in old-taxonomy snapshots (cnx500 2010-2015 style) map old->current where the mapping is unambiguous, else stay unknown. The first classification is effective from the chain's first bar; later reclassifications append. The loader (`load_industries`) stops dating a first classification at load time. Deliver an idempotent CLI with dry-run that writes hat_industry; do not write hat_live yourself: the PR body gives the exact command and the dry-run counts (members covered, unknown member-day share 2016+), and the orchestrator applies it.
2. Data audit: an `industries` check (warn when >5% of member-days resolve to unknown; fail when the run's policy has max_positions_per_industry < max_open_positions while coverage is incomplete; unknown share in run.json `data`); a store-wide 5m last-close vs daily close scan replacing the 120-day sample (F1); flag demerger factors whose ex-day open gap and close gap differ >15% and were not corrected (F3); count undated demergers on member chains in the run's window (F4).
