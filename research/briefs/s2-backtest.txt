S2 episodic pivot and 5-minute bars in the backtest, branch hat/s2-backtest. Goal:
1. Runs use the stored 5-minute bars. Today load_market never fills MarketData.intraday, so every run decides on daily estimates even where bars_5m has the session (2021-10-26..2026-10-23, 89.8% of NIFTY 500 member-days in 2022+). Where a session's bars are stored, the entries of that session follow the live trigger: the breakout level is max(consolidation_high, opening_range_high), the break is a close-confirmed 5m bar, volume_pace is evaluated (open item m3: DailyInputs.avg50_volume is unset in the loop), the low-of-day stop comes from the real pre-break low, and a gap over the level is entered instead of skipped (gap_over_no_intraday). Measured on 482 S2 breakout triggers in 2022+: the 09:15 bar is above the consolidation high on 28%, 19% never fire live, and the live entry is a median 0.36% higher. A run records, per year, the share of entries decided on 5m bars and on the daily estimate; the known gaps it records match what it actually approximated. A run can be forced to daily-only, so the two models compare on one window. It fits the 8 GiB box (load only what a session's entries and stops need).
2. The episodic pivot is backtested (today `episodic_pivot_not_backtested`: the scan skips pre-open playbooks, so EP never trades). Each session, a pre-open playbook gets candidates as live's 09:16 gap scan picks them: open vs previous close at least its gap_up min_pct, the bot's universe and the EP setup (neglect) on data to the previous session, and the bot's regime gate (live's GateBoard applies it to every trigger). Trigger, entry filters and the low-of-day stop are the live rules (opening-range-high break with close confirmation, early_volume, adtr_filter, max_chase) on the session's 5m bars. Without them, use a daily estimate calibrated on the 5m years and record it as a known gap. On gap days in 2022+ the 09:15 high sits a median 0.9% over the open, the low before the trigger a median 1.5% under it, and the trigger comes at a median 09:35 (90th percentile 11:45); a naive "high > open + 0.5%" proxy fires on 80% of gap days against 56% for the real trigger. catalyst_attached cannot be evaluated (hat_announcement is empty and there are no results dates): record it as a known gap, with gap plus early volume as the proxy. Replace `episodic_pivot_not_backtested` with codes that name what was approximated, and decide which block promotion (a 5m-decided EP entry whose only gap is the catalyst should not). Run folders report EP signals, refusals and trades per playbook, like the breakout's.
3. The 5m store keeps gap days. bars_basis.check_day rejects a day whose 5m high or low misses the raw daily bar's. The daily open is the pre-open auction price, and rows before 09:15 are dropped, so a stock that opened at its high on a gap fails. Of 580 NIFTY 500 member days 2021-11..2026-10 gapping >= 6% (neglected, Rs 5 cr turnover), 235 have no 5m bars, 198 of them basis_mismatch. The rejected ones opened at their high 41% of the time (kept: 14%) and closed a median 3.2% under the open (kept: 0.7%), so an EP backtest on this store keeps the winners and misses the fades. Make such days storable without weakening the raw-basis guarantee (a mismatch the official open fully explains), and report how many ledgered rejects would now pass. The refetch (Fyers minute_history; the Kotak raw files are deleted) is the orchestrator's step after the owner's go.
4. EP parameters behave the same live and in the backtest. The gap scan's floor is hard-coded at 10% (data/gapscan.py EP_MIN_GAP_PCT); it follows the active bots' gap_up min_pct, and each playbook still applies its own. adr_min can sit in a playbook's setup stage (today it is universe-only, so it filters EP candidates too: of 145 neglected 10% gappers passing Rs 10 cr, ADR >= 4% keeps 79), with the same result live, in the nightly scan and in the backtest.
