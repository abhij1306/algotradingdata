Scope:
1. A rotation strategy type in the engine (backend/app/hat/engine/strategy config + backtest/loop.py), config-driven, so a portfolio file can declare it like S1/S2 (docs/backtests/process.md "How to define a backtest"). Needed, per the REPORT's "Engine gap":
   - calendar rebalance (month_end | fortnight | weekly), decide on that session's close, execute at the next session's open (open auction fill rules as the engine already uses for opens; respect auction_days / suspensions)

   - exits: rank-based (fell out of buffer or ineligible); demerger forced exits and suspensions as today; no stop required. Rotation must opt out of stop-based risk caps, slots and per-trade risk sizing; S1/S2 behaviour unchanged (prove with resolved-config hashes as #308 did)

   - run folder per docs/backtests/result-standard.md (trades, equity, signals/rebalances, metrics); trade records carry the rank/score at entry and exit
