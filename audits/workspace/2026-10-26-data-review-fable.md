# Data review, hat_live + hat-data-isin, 2026-10-26 (independent, read-only)

Scope: the canonical market data a daily-bar NIFTY 500 swing backtest reads from 2016-01-01 (warm-up from 2011-06-22), plus the 5-minute store. Every check below was run by me against the stores and the original sources (NSE bhavcopy zips and `ind_close_all` files under `hat-data-isin/raw`), not taken from the code's docs or its audit. Nothing was written except this report, the built-in audit's own output file (`backtests/data_audits/aacdec6f….json`, written by the tool I was asked to run) and the golden sample `hat-data-isin/audits/golden/2026-10-26.json`. No `.env` was read.

## Verdict

**Trustworthy for daily-bar backtests from 2016, with fixes.** Daily bars, point-in-time membership, lineage, index series, features and breadth are sound (details and counts below). Two things must be fixed before intraday (5-minute) backtests, and one tooling bug must be fixed before anyone relies on `data.audit` again:

1. 3,880 stored 5-minute days (668 of them NIFTY 500 member-days, 14 names) carry Fyers' split/bonus-adjusted basis instead of the raw basis the store promises. Daily-bar runs do not read `bars_5m` (`used_by_run: false`), so this does not bias them.
2. `python -m app.hat.data.audit` crashes on main since #278 (today): the bare `SET TRANSACTION READ ONLY` has no bind under the two-database session. The audit cannot run as shipped.
3. SINTEX 2017-05-25 and CROMPGREAV 2016-03-15 demerger factors are taken at a thin ex-day open; pre-demerger history is scaled 44% / 20% off the ex-day close.

## Findings, by severity

### F1 (high, intraday only): 3,880 5-minute days stored on the adjusted basis
Evidence: full-store scan of `arg_max(close)` per (instrument, day) vs `bars_daily_raw.close`. Examples: BHAGYANGR 2025-10-23 5m close 8.58 vs daily 100.76 (ratio 0.0858 = stored later factors 0.0858); HDFCAMC 2025-10-23..2025-11-25 ratio 0.4986–0.5024 (23 member-days; its 2025-11-26 action); BRIGADE 159 member-days ratio 0.75; CUB 156 (0.75); ANANDRATHI 149 (0.50); IRB 107 (0.50); AIIL 56 (0.20); HEG 11 (0.356); plus JSL, JSWHL, GODREJIND, POLYMED, ECLERX, GRINDWELL, CGCL one day each (5–7%). Off-member: 3,212 days on 136 names. All stored, none in `bars_5m_rejects`; the audit's 120-day sample caught 2.
Why it matters: the EP/5m-confirm backtests read these days; a close-confirm or opening range would be computed on prices 2–12× off, or 25% off, for those names.
Cause (hypothesis, for the builder): these days come from the `minute_history` fetch (2025-10-22..2026-10-21, all NSE names, fetched 2026-10-22) and were "kept as stored" (63,932 days) by the later `bars5m_import` run. `check_day` with tolerance 0.005 against `bars_daily_raw_latest` cannot pass a 0.5 ratio, so either the conversion and check ran before those actions were stored and the days were written by a path that skips the check, or the tolerance was overridden. Needs a code read of `_flush`/`rebase_stored`.
Fix: add a store-wide 5m-vs-daily close scan to `data.audit` (not a 120-day sample); rebase or re-import the 3,880 days (`minute_history --rebase-into` with `--fetched-on 2026-10-22`, or re-import from the bars5m source files), then re-scan. List: `scratchpad/cmp5m.parquet` columns (instrument_id, date, r).

### F2 (high, tooling): `app.hat.data.audit` cannot run since #278
`audit.main` does `session.execute(text("SET TRANSACTION READ ONLY"))`; `HatSession.get_bind` finds no table in a bare `text()` and raises `UnboundExecutionError`. Reproduced with the exact command the owner uses. The earlier SINTEX warning came from a pre-#278 run (`52396357….json`). Fix: execute it on the live connection (`session.connection(bind_arguments={"bind": live_engine})` or `bind=`), and add a smoke test that runs `audit.main` against an empty universe.

### F3 (medium): demerger factors taken at a thin ex-day open (SINTEX, CROMPGREAV)
SINTEX 2017-05-25: prev close 104.10; ex-day open 17.90, low 16.15, high 34.00, close 25.85, volume 187.6M (bhavcopy). Stored factor 0.17195 = open gap; the close gap is 0.2483, so every pre-demerger adjusted price is 31% too low (the audit's +44% jump). CROMPGREAV 2016-03-15: factor 0.3413 (open) vs 0.2833 (close), pre-history 20% too high. Five more demergers differ 11–15% between open and close gap (SIEMENS 2025-04-07, NMDC 2022-10-27, ALLCARGO 2025-11-12, ABIRLANUVO 2016-01-20, ADANIENT 2018-04-05); those read as genuine ex-day moves. Backtest impact: positions exit before the ex-date (CA8), so only post-ex features (SMA150/200, 52-week high) for ~a year are off: SINTEX was no longer a member (left 2017-03-31), CROMPGREAV was. Fix: for demergers where the ex-day close gap differs > 15% from the open gap, store the factor at the ex-day close (or VWAP) and record the choice; recompute features for the two chains.

### F4 (medium): point-in-time quality of action dates
- All 242 bonuses and 158 of 159 splits on member chains have `known_on` = ex_date (none earlier); all 68 demergers have `known_on` ≈ ex_date + 6. Live would know splits weeks ahead (`hat_corporate_action.announced_on` is null on all dividends and 25 demergers). The backtest therefore never has a "factor pending" window before an ex-date; a position rides into the ex-date and is rescaled on it. Harmless for splits/bonuses (rescale is price-neutral) but it means the backtest cannot model the live `position.split_gap` lock or an announced-but-not-ex demerger except through `announced_on`, which 25 demergers lack (10 more are dated on/after ex): those positions hold through the demerger at adjusted prices (child shares not modelled). Fix: date the undated demergers from NSE's archived `bc` files or press releases where available; leave null where unknown (owner rule), but count the undated member demergers in each run report.
- CCAVENUE 2026-10-19 split (factor 10, consolidation) has null `known_on`; one of the two `bar_5m_off_daily` audit items is this name.

### F5 (low): breadth universe drifts
`breadth_pct` ranks all NSE ordinary equities with bars (1,593 names on 2019-03-15, 1,919 on 2023-10-20, ~2,500 in 2026) with fixed regime thresholds (40/50%). More small listings in later years changes what 50% means. Not a data error; a design note for the regime work (consider NIFTY 500 members or a size-filtered set).

### F6 (low): minor coverage facts
- RELINFRA 22 missing member-days 2025-12-22..2026-01-30 (absent from NSE bhavcopy: suspension). A held position would be marked at the stale close; check the engine's suspension path handles a 22-session gap.
- EMBASSY and BIRET (REITs) are NIFTY 500 members from 2026-09-30 with no bars at all (the ingest keeps only series EQ/BE/BZ with an ISIN). Never tradable; universe_size for the regime is unaffected.
- `hat_instrument.delisted_on` is NULL for all 4,422 instruments (open item D4).
- 5-minute store from 2026-07-17: 13,303 09:15 bars rejected (`ohlc`, 709 symbols, 68 days); 2026-10-23 has 564 of 617 names with a 09:15 bar. From 2026-08-03 ~80–100 names/day (200 of 617 on 10-22/23) lack the 15:15–15:25 bars (confirmed: 1,352/1,352 had 15:15 on 07-31; 1,269/1,348 on 08-03; 423/617 on 10-23). Both are source-side changes in the Fyers feed; the daily close is unaffected, the 5m day close is the 15:10 bar for those names.
- Dividends: 19,466 rows, no duplicates, cash > 0; INFY/TCS/RELIANCE counts per year look complete; 25 long-standing members have no dividend row ever (DBREALTY, SUZLON, HCC, ITI, …: plausible non-payers).
- Bars start 2011-06-22 although raw zips go back to 2010-04-01: the NSE bhavcopy gained its ISIN column on 2011-06-22 (verified in the zips), so earlier days cannot be ISIN-keyed. 4.5 years of warm-up before 2016 is enough for every feature (max window 252).
