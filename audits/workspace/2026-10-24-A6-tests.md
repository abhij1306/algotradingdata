# tests audit: backend/tests/hat  (2026-10-24, main @ 1a7aa75)

## Summary
The suite has been cut back to small tests, as CLAUDE.md asks. The cutting stopped halfway, though. Most of the world tests are gone, but the pure rules they used to cover never got small tests of their own. The biggest case is the one risk authority: `evaluate_signal` and every cap in `risk/caps.py` have no test at all. Several live order-path rules have none either: `route_mode`, `real_orders_allowed`, `scale_position`, `broker_trigger`, `cancel_reason` and `broker_quantities`. The deletions left residue behind: about 40 empty section headers, 73 dead module-level helpers, rigs nothing calls (`ExecEnv`, `live_session`, `hat_risk_support.decide`), and docstrings that still describe coverage that is gone. `FakeBroker` is a 323-line broker simulation, and the tests use only its failure injection. Fix first: give the risk caps and `evaluate_signal` table tests (F1). They gate every entry and nothing checks them.

### F1. The risk authority and its caps have no test   [High] [Confirmed] [Strong]
Files: backend/app/hat/risk/caps.py:48-119, backend/app/hat/risk/service.py (evaluate_signal), backend/tests/hat/properties/test_caps.py:38,41,72,234,263,266, backend/tests/hat/hat_risk_support.py:1-5,104
Problem: Twelve cap functions have no test anywhere under tests/hat (checked by name): `global_open_risk_ok`, `positions_ok`, `industry_ok`, `entries_ok`, `stopouts_ok`, `cooldown_ok`, `sessions_since`, `regime_gate_open`, `stop_valid`, `chase_ok`, `effective_max_chase_pct` and `is_loser`. `evaluate_signal`, the only caller of `size_position`, is never called by a test. `hat_risk_support.decide()` (:104) wraps it, and no test uses that wrapper. `test_caps.py` still has the section headers "global open risk", "per-bot caps", "the regime gate", "no adding to a position" and "total, typed, fail closed", each with nothing under it. Its docstring says it runs "on the real evaluate_signal". The only risk tests left cover sizing, kill thresholds, marks and policy-change direction. An off-by-one in any of these would pass CI: `<` against `<=` in `positions_ok`, `global_open_risk_ok` or `chase_ok`, a dropped `locked_upper`, or `stop_valid` allowing `stop == entry`. ### F2. Live order-path state rules with no small test   [High] [Confirmed] [Strong]
Files: backend/app/hat/execution/modes.py:166 (real_orders_allowed; callers submit.py:231, router.py:205), modes.py:205 (route_mode; caller engine/entries.py:219), backend/app/hat/engine/rescale.py:172-192 (scale_qty, scale_position; callers rescale.py:453, positions.py:105), backend/app/hat/execution/stop_plans.py:58 (broker_trigger; callers protect.py:376,726,756, gtt_upkeep.py:244), backend/app/hat/execution/entry_timeout.py:60-80 (break_failed, cancel_reason), backend/app/hat/execution/reconcile.py:92 (broker_quantities; callers reconcile.py:325, manage.py:666), backend/app/hat/execution/kite_payloads.py:216 (stop_is_below), backend/app/hat/execution/submit.py:241 (_lookup_before_retry)
Problem: Each of these is a pure function, or close to one, on a path that places, sizes, protects or reconciles real money, and none of them appears in any test. `route_mode` is the rule that auto places only high-confidence signals; if it inverts, low-confidence entries go straight to the broker in auto mode. `real_orders_allowed` is the HAT-EXEC-012 GTT gate. `scale_position` sets the held quantity (⌊q/f⌋) and floors both stops on a split; a wrong rounding direction breaks `current_stop >= initial_stop`, which the DB trigger then refuses at the ex-date open. `broker_trigger` rounds every broker stop to the tick. `broker_quantities` feeds the reconciliation that blocks an account. `test_rescale.py` covers only `exact_factor` and `split_like`, and its docstring still claims the quantity and stop rescale. `_lookup_before_retry` holds the "an entry is never sent again" rule (HAT-BRK-102). Only `OrderReconciliation.tla` covers it, and `test_conf_verification` checks that the function exists, not what it does.
Fix: Add small example tables for `route_mode`, `real_orders_allowed`, `scale_position`/`scale_qty` (including the invariant `current_stop >= initial_stop` after scaling, as a Hypothesis property), `broker_trigger` (never above the stop, on the tick, stop returned when the tick is unknown or ≤ 0), `cancel_reason` (break failed against timeout, the bar exactly at `triggered_at`), `broker_quantities` (non-CNC ignored, holding plus net) and `stop_is_below`. For `_lookup_before_retry`, pull the decision out as a pure `retry_verdict(purpose, found, filled_qty)` and test it. Optionally fold `broker_trigger` into `engine.stops.floor_to_tick`, which is already tested.
Tests: as listed. Each is a few lines and needs no database.
Size: M

### F3. Seeded entry criteria with no test   [Medium] [Confirmed] [Strong]

### F5. FakeBroker is a broker simulation the tests barely use, and it drifts from FyersBroker   [Medium] [Confirmed] [Worth exploring]
Files: backend/tests/hat/fakes/broker.py:1-323, backend/app/hat/execution/fyers_broker.py:99-103,178-212,755-781, backend/app/hat/execution/fyers_payloads.py:428-443, backend/tests/hat/properties/test_tags.py:387-454, test_broker.py:123,176, backend/tests/hat/conformance/test_conf_authorities.py:9-11, docs/architecture.md:60
Problem: `FakeBroker` models scripted outcome queues, book lag (`lagging`), landed-but-unconfirmed places, modify and cancel rewriting the book, a live-shaped GTT book with `resting_gtts`, and `calls_named`. No test uses `script`, `lagging`, `landed=True`, `modify_reply`, `cancel_reply`, `place_gtt_reply`, `resting_gtts` or `calls_named`. Two tests construct it in test_broker.py, and test_tags.py uses it for failure injection (circuit, raise, garbage, place_reply). The tests that drove the simulation are gone, so the simulation has nothing to stay honest against. It already drifts from the real `FyersBroker` in two ways:
- `cancel_gtt` ignores `cancel_reply` and always answers `{"s": "ok"}` unless scripted, while `FyersBroker.cancel_gtt` returns the timeout fallback or Fyers' error (fyers_broker.py:778).
- The fake's replies carry no `details` (`accepted()`) or `details: None` (`rejected()`), while every real `OrderReply` carries the raw reply in `details` (fyers_broker.py:202-206, 212). `place_result`'s rate-limited branch (fyers_payloads.py:441) can never be reached through the fake.

`RecordingSdk` plus `broker_over()` already runs the real `FyersBroker` classification over a fake SDK, and its method names match `fyers_apiv3.FyersModel` exactly. That is the better seam. `docs/architecture.md:60` calls FakeBroker "the only fake", but it fakes the Fyers transport, not `HatBroker`. With `HAT_BROKER=zerodha`, `KiteHatBroker`'s own branches have no fake at all: lookup failure goes to rejected, a raise goes to unconfirmed, and a trigger at or above the LTP goes to rejected (kite_adapter.py:244-301).
Fix: Shrink `FakeBroker` to the failure injection test_tags uses, or move those tests onto `RecordingSdk` (raise, error payload, garbage rows) and delete `FakeBroker` and its authority entry. Add a recording Kite client with the same failure kinds for `KiteHatBroker`'s branches.
Tests: test_tags' fail-closed tests running over `broker_over(RecordingSdk(...))`, plus a `test_kite_adapter_branches` over a recording Kite client.
