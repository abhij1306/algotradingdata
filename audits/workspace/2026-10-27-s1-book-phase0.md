## 3. Setup score (mode score, threshold 0.6)

engine/strategy/scoring.py:79-118. Hard criteria stop the stage on failure; score criteria add (weight, score); stage passes when every hard passed and the weighted mean ≥ threshold. In ride the only enabled score criterion is `vcp` (weight 2), so score = vcp score.
- A failed VCP returns `verdict(False, …)` → score 0 (base.py:73-79) → 0 < 0.6 → `below_threshold`. A failing VCP also publishes no `pivot`, so `close_vs_pivot` (hard, order 12) fails `no_pivot` first (setup.py:368-370).
- A passing VCP scores ≥ (1 + 0.5 + 0.5 + 0.5)/4 = 0.625 (vcp.py:170-189, `score_floor` 0.5).
- **So no: a failed VCP cannot pass.** "Weak" VCP = whatever passes the (loose) rules above; the score adds no filter beyond them.

## 4. `ambiguous=optimistic`

The prompt's case (stop and target in one bar) is **already pessimistic** by construction: a held day is read open → low → high → close (loop.py:635-662): the stop is tested on the way down (`_down_leg`, loop.py:593-605, `stop_fill` = min(open, stop), fills.py:92-97) before any profit rule on the way up (`_up_leg`, loop.py:607-633). The entry day takes the post-break low before the high too (loop.py docstring :50-56).
The `ambiguous` knob covers something else: a stop **raised on the way up** (step trail, breakeven) whose day low reaches the raised stop while the close stays above (fills.py:100-105, loop.py:1341-1351). Optimistic keeps the position; pessimistic fills at the raised stop.
In ride nothing raises a stop intraday (step trail, breakeven off): `ambiguous_days` = 0 (evidence.json). **Switching it is a no-op for ride**; it binds in s1-book-01 (breakeven at +2.5R).

## 5. Trigger on daily data

- Level = `pivot` via `trigger.daily_level` (trigger.py:481-487, loop.py:1083-1086).
- Entry when the daily **high** reaches the first tick above the pivot (`penetration_1_tick`), fill at pivot + 1 tick (fills.py:65-89, loop.py:1088-1099). `bar: 5m` and `confirm: close` are **ignored** on daily days, and so is `volume_pace` (spec.py:105-111 `trigger_is_level_break_only`).
- Gap-up (open > pivot): fill **at the open** (fills.py:78-79); `max_chase` then rejects if open > pivot × 1.02 or locked upper circuit (live.py:292-301, loop.py:1136-1160). So gaps beyond 2% are already skipped (ride: 2 `max_chase` refusals).
- Since #293 (after ride) the default is `entry_bars: bars_5m` (spec.py:127-130): a "re-run unchanged" must pass `--entry-bars daily`.

## 6. by_year attribution — reporting defect

backtest/folder.py:398-438: `return_pct` is mark-to-market equity (last close vs previous year's), but `trades` and `expectancy_r` are the trades **exiting** that year (:419, :428). Unweighted R mean, crumb positions count equally.
Run 01a17ca4, recomputed from trades.csv:
- 2017: 31 exits, mean R 0.0016, realized P&L ₹18.7k, but +66.9% MTM. VAKRANGEE (entry 2017-01-12, exit 2018-01-31, +41.5R, ₹4.61L) was marked up through 2017. By entry year 2017: 35 trades, mean R +1.32.
- 2018: 19 exits, mean R 2.31 is mostly VAKRANGEE's +41.5R; the stock's January 2018 fall plus the year's losers give −11.4% MTM. By entry year 2018: 16 trades, mean R −0.15.
Not an arithmetic error, but two different bases in one row with no label: the table reads as contradictory and misattributes multi-year winners. **Treating as a bug.** Proposed fix: keep MTM return; add `trades_entered`, `expectancy_r_by_entry`, `trades_exited`, `expectancy_r_by_exit`, `realized_pnl` per year, all named by basis; test with a trade spanning a year end.

## 7. Slot order and capital

`slot_order=time` is **not** first-come by signal date: it is the estimated intraday trigger order within a day: gap-overs first, then (pivot − open)/open ascending, ties by setup score (backtest/slots.py:43-76, loop.py:804-811). Each entry then takes cash in that order; one short of cash is refused `size_zero_cash` or `size_below_min` (ride: 150 and 209). `score` and `random:K` exist; no RS-rank order. A `for_promotion` run refuses any order but `time` (loop.py docstring :36-38).
