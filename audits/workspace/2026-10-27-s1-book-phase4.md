## How the audit reads the run

The script replays the run's nightly scan without the day loop: the run's `config.json` through
`runs._replayed`, the bot's universe each night (`watchlist.bot_universe_members`), contexts and
the eligible RS rank as `scan.scan_bot` builds them, and `evaluate_playbook` on every stock-day.
**Its funnel equals `run.json` `funnel` gate for gate** (531,500 evaluated → 293,828 universe →
110,768 template → 63,625 RS → 1,299 geometry → 1,009 → 570 → 295 → 288 → 203 close_vs_pivot;
425 regime-off nights; script output "funnel matches run.json funnel"). The script stops on any
mismatch, on any stock-day where `book_vcp` and the diagnostic disagree about C (none of the
63,625 RS-passing stock-days did), and on any base number that differs from `book_vcp`'s.
The 12 entries' footprint, contraction depths and base number all equal `trades.csv`
(`audit.md`, "Entries checked against trades.csv": E01..E12 yes), and their mean R is 0.4966, the
run's expectancy.

## Seed and sampling

- Seed **20261027** (`DEFAULT_SEED`, printed as `seed 20261027`).
- Entries: min(20, n) with n = 12, so **all 12 trades are audited** (trade ids 1..12).
- Near-misses: stock-days with signal date 2016-01-01..2021-09-30, regime-on nights, in the bot's
  universe (NIFTY500_PIT + turnover + price, the universe stage), passing the trend template's
  eight checks, with an H0, and failing exactly one rule of C, each rule evaluated on its own
  (`book_diagnostic.geometry_rules`). The sorted pool is shuffled with the seed and the first
  stock-day of each symbol is taken until 10 (`pick_near_misses`). Pool size by failing rule:

| rule | stock-days |
|---|---|
| base_length | 111 |
| swing_high_above_h0 | 0 |
| contraction_count | 0 |
| first_depth_band | 52 |
| depth_ratio | 5,391 |
| final_depth | 612 |
| final_sessions | 545 |
| **total** | **6,711** |

`swing_high_above_h0` can never fail: H0 is the highest high of the 325 sessions ending on the
signal date (`book.py:211`), so no later swing high can exceed it. `contraction_count` never
fails alone: a base with one contraction or more than six also fails another rule.

## Chart review: failure modes

I looked at all 22 charts. Each chart marks H0, every swing high (Hn) and swing low (Ln) the
detector used, the trailing unconfirmed swing high in grey, the pivot and stop. In most cases the
marks sit on the visible swing highs and lows. These are the cases where they do not, or where
the marked contractions don't describe the visible base:

**F1. Contraction low on a bar the zigzag never marked** (E11, N01, N02, N09). The zigzag
(`book.py:196-207`) ignores the low of a bar that sets a new high (line 203), and while it
follows highs it never treats a low as a swing-low candidate. A swing-low candidate starts only
on the bar that confirms the swing high (line 207). `contractions_of` then measures the depth
from the lowest low of all bars Hi..Li (`book.py:250`). So the depth comes from one bar and
the contraction's end (Li) is on a later one.
- E11 BSOFT (an entry): the low behind Depth_1 12.85% is 367.20 on the H0 bar itself
  (2021-06-09). Swing low L1 is the next day at 381.55, so contraction 1 lasts 2 sessions. On
  the chart, L2 (375.00) looks like an undercut of L1, but `shakeout` (`book.py:513`) compares
  contraction lows (367.20) and logs none.
- N09 NATIONALUM: C3's low is 71.20 on 2021-05-31, but the swing low is 2021-06-04 (71.50).
  The final contraction's end, its length and its dry-up window (`dryup_ratios`) are 4
  sessions late.
- N01 OIL C3 (low 143.58 on the swing-high bar 2017-02-07) and N02 KWALITY C3 (135.10 on the
  swing-high bar 2017-05-31): the same mechanism.

**F2. The base's lowest low is outside every contraction** (N03). ICICIBANK's chart shows a low
of 287.00 on 2017-12-18, below L1 (298.15), and no contraction includes it. Between L2
(2017-12-13) and H3 (2017-12-22) there is no swing: the bar that confirmed L2 is checked only
against the high side (`book.py:200-202`), so its own low never enters the swing list. Depth_1 is
10.29%, but the visible correction from H0 (332.35) is 13.6% deep, and no shakeout is logged.

**F3. Final swing low undercut before the signal** (N09, N10). The decline is not confirmed yet,
so it is not a contraction (spec C: confirmed swings only). Pivot and stop come from the earlier
contraction, which is stale, and the would-be stop is already above the signal-day price.
- N09: low 69.75 on 2021-06-09, below final L 71.50; stop 70.84.
- N10 HAPPSTMNDS: low 1400.95 on 2021-09-21, below final L 1461.00; stop 1453.69.

A related case is N08 COCHINSHIP. A trailing swing high of 201.82 (2021-06-07, grey) is above
the pivot (201.25) the night before the signal. `close_vs_pivot` reads the close, so the base
still counts as unbroken.

The following are not marking errors (the marks match the bars) but limit what the detected
"contraction" means:

**O1. Tightening that comes only from a lower high after a one-session spike at H0** (E07, E08,
E12; spike H0 also on E01, E04, E05, E06, E09, E10). The contraction lows are flat, and depth
falls only because H0 is a one-bar wick above the range:
- JKCEMENT: L1 2725.0, L2 2726.4 (H0 3149.8, H2 2967.6).
- DEEPAKNTR: L1 1712, L2 1718.
- BALAMINES: L1 3169.7, L2 3180.0.

These are descending-top, flat-bottom ranges rather than rising lows. The spec does not ask for
rising lows.

**O2. A first contraction of 2-3 sessions** (E11: 2 sessions; E09 CHAMBLFERT: 3 sessions, H0
2021-05-20 to L1 2021-05-24). Most of Depth_1 is the H0 bar's own range. The spec gives only
the final contraction a minimum length.

**O3. Minimum 3% swings split one leg into extra contractions, and `depth_ratio` then rejects
the base** (N01, N02, N04, N05, N07, N08, N10). A brief pause inside the recovery or inside the
first decline counts as a contraction. When the next contraction is deeper, the base fails:
- N04 BAJAJHLDNG: C3 4.04% over 2 sessions, then C4 7.06%.
- N05 HINDUNILVR: C4 3.41%, then C5 7.74%.
- N08 COCHINSHIP: C3 5.08%, then C4 10.98%.
- N01 OIL: C2 5.45%, then C3 5.50%.

This is why `depth_ratio` is 80% of the near-miss pool (5,391 of 6,711).

**O4. H0 before the real correction** (N07 ASHOKLEY). C1 is a 13.17% pullback from H0. The
deeper fall (C2, 19.61%) starts from H2, which is 3% below H0, so the base fails `depth_ratio`.

Outcomes, for reference only: 5 of the 10 near-misses closed above the pivot within 20 sessions.
Their 60-session MFE was 1.05-4.87R, and four of them (N03, N06, N07, N09) reached at least 4R.
Ten stock-days are too few to read anything from this.

(Failures by rule are in `audit.md`. A stock-day can fail several rules.)

**Responsible rule: F, relative correction** (`book.py:371-377`, spec F). 275 stock-days with
Depth_1 ≥ 15% pass C, D and E (178 + 47 + 50), and **none passes F**. Taken on its own, F rejects
636 of the 641 deeper bases that pass C (99.2%) and none of the 658 in 10-15%. F rejects a base
when Depth_1 > 15% and Depth_1 > 2.5 × idx_depth, so a 15% base needs the NIFTY 500 to have
fallen at least 6% between H0 and the signal, and a 25% base at least 10%. In-sample, the
median fall over those windows was 2.0-3.5%. Below 15% the rule is off (`rc_min_depth_pct`), so
shallow bases pass at any ratio: entries E09 and E10 had rc_ratio 15.4 and 16.5.
- C does not cause the clustering: its pass rate is about the same in every bucket (4.6-7.3%).
- D removes few deep bases.
- E removes more in 20-25% (65% fail the mean dry-up) but leaves 50 of 61 in 25-35%.
- G removes almost none.

So the spec's candidate is confirmed with numbers: rule F alone removes every deeper base before
an entry is possible. This is a finding, not a parameter change.
